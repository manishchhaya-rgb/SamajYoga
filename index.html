Tip: place your device so the camera sees you head-to-toe. Good, even lighting and a plain background help the tracker. Nothing you see here is uploaded — the video and pose detection stay on your machine.

🟥🟩 Skeleton colours: every body line the pose checks starts red and turns green as soon as that part is in alignment — e.g. the line across your shoulders goes green when your shoulders are level. When every checked line is green you're at 100%.

🔊 Voice coaching: click the button to have the app speak your corrections aloud — handy when you're in a pose and can't look at the screen. It speaks the most important fix first, waits a few seconds between cues so it isn't chatty, and praises you when your alignment is good.

If you see a "model couldn't download" message: the app needs to fetch the pose model from the internet once. Some networks/firewalls block this when the file is opened directly. The reliable fix is to serve the file from a tiny local server: open a terminal in the folder with this file and run python3 -m http.server 8000, then visit http://localhost:8000/yoga-teacher.html in Chrome.
