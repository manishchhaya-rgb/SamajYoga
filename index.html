    ❓ Help & tips
    Set up: place your device so the camera sees you head-to-toe. Good, even lighting and a plain background help the tracker. Nothing is uploaded — the video and pose detection stay on your device.

    The outline: each pose is drawn on screen as a see-through outline. Stand where your body fits inside it — the app tells you to step back, come closer, or move left/right. The outline turns green when you're inside it, and it flips to match the side you're facing.

    Facing the camera or side-on: each pose tells you (on screen and out loud) whether to face the camera or stand side-on. If you're turned the wrong way, an orange prompt appears until you turn.

    Skeleton colours: every body line a pose checks starts red and turns green when that part is aligned. When every checked line is green you're at 100%.

    Hold timer: once every checked line is green the timer counts down. If you slip out of alignment it pauses and carries on when you're back in position. In single-pose practice change the time with − / +.

    My sequence: press Start sequence and the app coaches each pose in turn, moving on 3 seconds after each hold. The default sequence does the facing-the-camera poses first, then the side-on ones, so you only turn once.

    Voice: on by default — it announces each pose and how to stand, then speaks the most important fix, waiting a few seconds between cues. Turn it off with the button at the top.

    If you see a "model couldn't download" message: the app needs to fetch the pose model from the internet once. Some networks/firewalls block this. Run a tiny local server instead: open a terminal in the folder with this file, run python3 -m http.server 8000, then visit http://localhost:8000/index.html in Chrome.
